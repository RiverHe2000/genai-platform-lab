# Data Retention and Privacy Standard

Retention. Customer records are retained for 7 years after the end of the customer
relationship, after which they are securely destroyed unless subject to a legal hold.
Transaction records are retained for 7 years from the date of the transaction.

Personal information. Personal information includes a customer's name in combination with
date of birth, residential address, tax file number (TFN), account numbers, biometric
identifiers and any information from which an individual is reasonably identifiable.

Tax file numbers. TFNs are stored only in encrypted fields of the customer master system.
They must never be recorded in free-text fields, emails, chat transcripts or application
logs, and access is restricted to staff with a tax-reporting role.

Data classification. Information is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or
Restricted. Customer personal information is Confidential by default and Restricted where
it includes TFNs, health information or biometric data.

Notifiable data breaches. A suspected eligible data breach must be assessed within 30
days. Where the breach is likely to result in serious harm, the Office of the Australian
Information Commissioner and the affected individuals are notified.

Access control. Access to Confidential and Restricted data is role-based and reviewed
quarterly. Access that has not been used for 90 days is revoked automatically.

Privacy impact assessments. A privacy impact assessment is required for any new system or
use case that processes personal information, including generative AI use cases.
