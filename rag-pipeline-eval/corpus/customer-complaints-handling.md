# Customer Complaints Handling

Acknowledgement. Every complaint is acknowledged within 1 business day of receipt and
recorded in the complaints register on the same day.

Resolution timeframes. Standard complaints must receive a final internal dispute
resolution (IDR) response within 30 calendar days. Complaints involving financial
hardship or a notice of default must be resolved within 21 calendar days.

Content of the response. The IDR response states the outcome, the reasons for it, and the
customer's right to escalate the complaint to the Australian Financial Complaints
Authority (AFCA) if they are not satisfied.

Escalation. Complaints that remain unresolved after the applicable timeframe, or where the
customer rejects the outcome, are referred to the Customer Advocate, who reviews the case
independently of the business unit.

Vulnerable customers. Complaints from customers identified as vulnerable are prioritised
and assigned to a specialist team.

Systemic issues. Complaints are analysed monthly for systemic issues. Systemic issues and
complaint volumes per 1,000 customers are reported quarterly to the Board Risk Committee.

Training. All frontline staff complete complaints-handling training annually.
