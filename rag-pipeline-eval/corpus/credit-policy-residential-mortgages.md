# Credit Policy: Residential Mortgages

Scope. This policy governs the origination of owner-occupied and investment residential
mortgage loans by Meridian Bank in Australia. It applies to new lending, top-ups and
refinances of existing loans.

Loan-to-value ratio. The maximum loan-to-value ratio (LVR) without lenders mortgage
insurance (LMI) is 80%. With LMI, the maximum LVR is 95% for owner-occupiers and 90% for
investors. LVR is calculated on the lower of the contract price and the bank-accepted
valuation.

Serviceability. Every application is assessed at the higher of the contract interest rate
plus a buffer of 3.0 percentage points, or the floor assessment rate of 5.5%. Living
expenses are taken as the higher of the applicant's declared expenses and the Household
Expenditure Measure (HEM) benchmark for the household profile. Rental income is shaded to
80% and bonus income to 70%.

Debt-to-income. Applications with a debt-to-income (DTI) ratio above 6 require Credit
Committee approval. A DTI above 8 is not permitted under any delegation.

Interest-only terms. Interest-only periods are limited to 5 years for owner-occupiers and
a cumulative 10 years over the life of the loan for investors. An interest-only
application is assessed on the principal-and-interest repayments over the residual term.

Deposit. Where the LVR exceeds 90%, the borrower must demonstrate genuine savings of at
least 5% of the purchase price held for a minimum of three months.

Exceptions. Any exception to this policy must be recorded in the origination system with
the approving delegate's name and reported monthly to the Credit Risk Committee.
