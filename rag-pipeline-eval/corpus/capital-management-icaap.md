# Capital Management and ICAAP

Capital ratios. The regulatory minimum Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1) ratio, including the
capital conservation buffer and the countercyclical buffer, is 10.25%. The Board has set a
management target range for CET1 of 11.0% to 11.5% and an internal trigger of 10.75%.
The minimum total capital ratio is 13.5%, and the minimum leverage ratio is 3.5%.

Actions at the trigger. If the CET1 ratio falls below the internal trigger of 10.75%,
ordinary dividends are suspended and the Chief Financial Officer presents a capital
restoration plan to the Board within one month.

ICAAP. The Internal Capital Adequacy Assessment Process (ICAAP) is prepared annually and
approved by the Board. It projects capital over a three-year horizon under the base case
and the enterprise stress scenarios, and it documents the capital allocated to each
material risk including credit, market, interest rate risk in the banking book,
operational and climate risk.

Capital planning. Capital is planned on a rolling three-year basis and updated quarterly.
Any planned acquisition or portfolio purchase above AUD 500 million requires a capital
impact assessment approved by the Board.

Reporting. The capital position is reported monthly to the Board Risk Committee and
quarterly to APRA in the prescribed returns.
