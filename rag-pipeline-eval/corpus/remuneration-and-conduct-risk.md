# Remuneration and Conduct Risk Policy

Deferral. Variable remuneration is deferred as follows: 6 years for the Chief Executive
Officer, 5 years for other senior executives and 4 years for other material risk-takers.
Deferred amounts vest no faster than pro rata.

Clawback. Vested variable remuneration may be clawed back for up to 2 years after vesting
where misconduct, a material risk management failure or a material misstatement is
identified.

Performance measures. Financial measures may account for no more than 50% of the weighting
in variable remuneration scorecards. Non-financial measures include risk management,
conduct, customer outcomes and compliance.

Consequence management. Conduct breaches are assessed by the Consequence Management
Committee, which may reduce or cancel unvested remuneration.

Conduct indicators. Conduct risk is monitored through indicators including complaints per
1,000 customers, breach counts, the number of substantiated whistleblower reports and
mandatory training completion rates.

Governance. The Board Remuneration Committee reviews the policy annually and approves the
variable remuneration outcomes for all material risk-takers.
