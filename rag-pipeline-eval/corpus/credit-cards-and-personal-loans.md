# Credit Policy: Credit Cards and Personal Loans

Personal loans. Unsecured personal loans range from AUD 2,000 to AUD 75,000 with terms
from 1 to 7 years. Applicants must have been in their current employment for at least 6
months or be self-employed for at least 2 years.

Credit cards. The minimum gross annual income for a credit card is AUD 25,000. The
initial credit limit for a new customer is capped at 3 times the applicant's gross monthly
income. Cards offer up to 55 interest-free days on purchases when the closing balance is
paid in full.

Bureau score cut-offs. Applications with a bureau score below 500 are declined.
Applications scoring from 500 to 599 are referred for manual assessment. Applications
scoring 600 or above are automatically approved provided serviceability passes.

Responsible lending. For credit cards, serviceability is assessed on the applicant's
ability to repay the full credit limit within 3 years. For personal loans, the assessment
uses the contractual repayment at the assessment rate.

Limit increases. Credit limit increases are offered only on the customer's request and
require a fresh serviceability assessment.

Hardship. Customers who notify financial hardship are referred to the Financial Assistance
team within 2 business days.
