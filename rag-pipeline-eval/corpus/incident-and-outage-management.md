# Technology Incident and Outage Management

Severity levels. Incidents are classified P1 (critical: a critical operation is
unavailable), P2 (major: significant degradation), P3 (minor) or P4 (cosmetic).

Response times. A P1 incident must be acknowledged and have a response team engaged
within 15 minutes, with status updates to stakeholders every 30 minutes until resolved. A
P2 incident must be responded to within 1 hour, with updates every 2 hours.

Recovery objectives. Critical systems have a recovery time objective (RTO) of 4 hours and
a recovery point objective (RPO) of 15 minutes. Non-critical systems have an RTO of 24
hours and an RPO of 4 hours.

Customer communication. Where a P1 incident affects customers, a customer notification is
published on the status page and app within 1 hour of the incident being declared.

Post-incident review. Every P1 and P2 incident is followed by a post-incident review
completed within 10 business days, with actions tracked to closure by the Technology Risk
Committee.

Regulatory link. Incidents that disrupt a critical operation beyond its tolerance level are
escalated to Operational Risk for APRA notification under the CPS 230 standard.
