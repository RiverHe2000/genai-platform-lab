# Third-Party and Vendor Risk Standard

Tiering. Vendors are tiered as Critical, Important or Standard based on the impact of a
service failure on critical operations, customers and data.

Due diligence. Critical vendors undergo an annual assessment covering financial viability,
information security, business continuity and sub-contracting. Important vendors are
assessed every two years and Standard vendors at contract renewal.

Contract requirements. Contracts with Critical vendors must include audit rights for the
bank and for APRA, a 90-day termination assistance period, data return and destruction
obligations, and notification of security incidents within 24 hours.

Concentration. No single cloud provider may host more than 70% of the bank's critical
workloads. Concentration is reported to the Board Risk Committee semi-annually.

Exit plans. Every Critical vendor has a documented exit plan that is reviewed annually and
tested at least every three years.

Sub-outsourcing. A Critical vendor may not sub-outsource a material part of the service
without the bank's prior written approval.
