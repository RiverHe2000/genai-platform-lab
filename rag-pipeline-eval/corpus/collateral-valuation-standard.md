# Collateral Valuation Standard

Residential property. An automated valuation model (AVM) is acceptable where the LVR is
60% or below and the loan is AUD 1 million or less. A desktop valuation by a panel valuer
is acceptable where the LVR is 80% or below. A full inspection valuation is required in all
other cases, and for any loan above AUD 2 million regardless of LVR.

Commercial property. Commercial property always requires a full valuation. Commercial
security is revalued at least every 3 years, and annually while the exposure is on the
watchlist.

Haircuts. For lending purposes the following haircuts are applied to market value:
residential property 20%, commercial property 30%, rural property 40%, listed shares 50%,
and cash deposits 0%.

Valuer panel. Valuations are ordered through the valuation management platform, which
rotates instructions across the panel. No single valuation firm may receive more than 30%
of instructions in a region in any quarter.

Currency of valuations. A residential valuation is valid for 90 days for the purpose of
settlement; older valuations must be refreshed.

Disputes. A borrower may request a review of a valuation once; the review is performed by
a different panel valuer.
