# Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Program

Customer identification. Customers are identified and verified before an account is
opened. Enhanced customer due diligence applies to politically exposed persons and to
customers connected with high-risk jurisdictions.

Threshold transaction reports. Cash transactions of AUD 10,000 or more are reported to
AUSTRAC within 10 business days.

Suspicious matter reports. A suspicious matter report is lodged with AUSTRAC within 3
business days of forming a suspicion, or within 24 hours where the suspicion relates to
terrorism financing.

Ongoing monitoring. Transaction monitoring rules run daily. The rule set is reviewed at
least semi-annually and after any material change in products or customer base.

Record keeping. Customer identification records and transaction records are retained for
7 years.

Independent review. The AML/CTF program is independently reviewed every 2 years by
Internal Audit or an external specialist.

Training. All staff complete AML/CTF training on induction and annually thereafter.
