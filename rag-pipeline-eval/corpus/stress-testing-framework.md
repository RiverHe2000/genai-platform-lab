# Stress Testing Framework

Frequency. An enterprise-wide stress test is performed annually, with scenarios approved by
the Board on the recommendation of the Board Risk Committee.

Severe downturn scenario. The severe downturn scenario assumes unemployment peaking at
10.5%, residential property prices falling by 30% from peak, GDP contracting by 4% and
the cash rate reduced to 0.1%. The scenario runs over a three-year horizon.

Outcome requirement. Under the severe downturn scenario the CET1 ratio must remain at or
above 8.0% in every year of the horizon after management actions. If the projection falls
below 8.0%, the capital plan must be revised before the ICAAP is approved.

Reverse stress testing. A reverse stress test is performed annually to identify the
combination of events that would breach regulatory minimum capital.

Use of results. Results feed the ICAAP, the risk appetite statement and the calibration of
portfolio concentration limits. Provisioning models are re-run under the stress scenarios
to assess the sensitivity of expected credit losses.

Model dependencies. The stress test relies on the PD, LGD and EAD models, the
pre-provision net revenue model and the IRRBB model; all are listed in the model
inventory with their validation status.
