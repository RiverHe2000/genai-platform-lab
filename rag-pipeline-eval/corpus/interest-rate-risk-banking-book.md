# Interest Rate Risk in the Banking Book

Measures. Interest rate risk in the banking book (IRRBB) is measured through the change in
economic value of equity (EVE) and the change in net interest income (NII).

Limits. The EVE sensitivity limit is 15% of Tier 1 capital under a parallel shock of plus
or minus 200 basis points. The NII sensitivity limit is 10% of forecast 12-month net
interest income under a parallel shock of plus or minus 100 basis points.

Scenarios. Six standard shock scenarios are run monthly: parallel up, parallel down,
steepener, flattener, short-rate up and short-rate down. Results are reported to ALCO.

Behavioural assumptions. Non-maturity deposits are split into core and non-core balances.
Core balances are assigned a weighted average life of 3 years, capped at 5 years.
Prepayment assumptions for fixed-rate mortgages are calibrated annually from observed
prepayment behaviour.

Hedging. Structural interest rate risk is hedged with interest rate swaps executed by
Treasury within the limits above. Hedge effectiveness is tested quarterly.

Model ownership. The IRRBB measurement model is a Tier 2 model owned by Treasury Finance.
