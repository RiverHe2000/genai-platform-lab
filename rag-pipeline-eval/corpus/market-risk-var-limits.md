# Market Risk: Value-at-Risk Limits and Backtesting

Measure. Trading-book market risk is measured with a one-day value-at-risk (VaR) at the
99% confidence level, computed by historical simulation over the most recent 250 trading
days with full revaluation.

Limits. The total trading-book VaR limit is AUD 5 million. Desk limits are AUD 2 million
for the foreign exchange desk and AUD 3 million for the rates desk. Utilisation is
reported daily, and a limit breach is escalated to the Chief Risk Officer on the same day.

Backtesting. Daily VaR is backtested against hypothetical (clean) profit and loss. Over a
rolling 250-day window, 0 to 4 exceptions place the model in the green zone, 5 to 9
exceptions in the amber zone, and 10 or more exceptions in the red zone. A red-zone
outcome raises the regulatory capital multiplier to 4.0 and triggers a model review.

Stressed VaR. Stressed VaR is calibrated to the 12-month window covering the 2008 to 2009
financial crisis and is recomputed weekly.

Sensitivities. In addition to VaR, the desks are subject to sensitivity limits: DV01 for
the rates desk and net open position by currency for the foreign exchange desk.

New products. Any new product must be approved by the New Product Committee, including
confirmation that the risk system can capture its sensitivities and that valuation models
have been validated.
