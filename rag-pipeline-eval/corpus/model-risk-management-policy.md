# Model Risk Management Policy

Purpose. This policy sets the minimum requirements for the development, validation,
approval, use and monitoring of models at Meridian Bank. It applies to statistical,
machine-learning and large-language-model based systems alike, consistent with APRA CPG
235 and the principles of SR 11-7.

Model tiering. Models are tiered by materiality. Tier 1 models drive regulatory capital,
provisioning or pricing decisions on portfolios with more than AUD 1 billion of exposure.
Tier 2 models inform material business decisions below that threshold. Tier 3 models are
low-impact analytical tools.

Validation frequency. Tier 1 models are independently validated annually, Tier 2 models
every two years and Tier 3 models every three years. Any model change that moves the
output on the benchmark portfolio by more than 10% triggers a full revalidation regardless
of the cycle.

Independence. Validation is performed by the Model Risk function in the second line of
defence. Model owners sit in the first line and may not validate their own models.

Model inventory. Every model in use must be registered in the model inventory with an
owner, a tier, the date of last validation and the date of the next scheduled validation.

Findings. Validation findings are rated High, Medium or Low. High findings must be
remediated within 90 days and Medium findings within 180 days; Low findings are tracked to
the next validation.

Committee. The Model Risk Committee meets monthly, is chaired by the Chief Risk Officer,
and approves new Tier 1 and Tier 2 models and any use of a model outside its approved
scope.
