# Climate Risk Management Standard

Risk types. Climate risk is managed as physical risk (acute events such as floods and
bushfires, and chronic changes such as sea-level rise) and transition risk (policy,
technology and market changes on the path to lower emissions).

Scenario analysis. Climate scenario analysis is performed annually using the Network for
Greening the Financial System (NGFS) Orderly, Disorderly and Hot House World scenarios
over horizons of 10 and 30 years.

Sector limits. Exposure to high-emitting sectors is limited to 8% of the corporate lending
book. No new financing is provided for thermal coal mining from 1 January 2026.

Physical risk in mortgages. Residential lending on properties in a designated flood zone
requires confirmation of flood insurance before settlement. Flood-exposed and
bushfire-exposed mortgage balances are reported quarterly to the Board Risk Committee.

Disclosure. Climate-related financial disclosures are prepared in line with AASB S2 from
the financial year beginning 1 July 2025.

Data. Emissions data for corporate borrowers is collected at origination and annual review,
and estimated with sector proxies where borrower data is unavailable.
