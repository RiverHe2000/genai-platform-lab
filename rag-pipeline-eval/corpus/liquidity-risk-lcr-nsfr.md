# Liquidity Risk Standard: LCR and NSFR

Liquidity coverage ratio. The regulatory minimum liquidity coverage ratio (LCR) is 100%.
Meridian Bank operates with an internal trigger of 110% and a management target of 115%.
Breaching the internal trigger requires the Treasurer to present a remediation plan to the
Asset and Liability Committee (ALCO) within two business days.

Net stable funding ratio. The regulatory minimum net stable funding ratio (NSFR) is 100%,
and the management target is 110%.

High-quality liquid assets. At least 60% of the stock of high-quality liquid assets (HQLA)
must be Level 1 assets: cash, central bank reserves and Australian Government and
semi-government securities. Self-securitised residential mortgages are held as
contingent collateral for the Reserve Bank of Australia but are not counted as HQLA.

Survival horizon. The bank must survive a 30-day combined market-wide and idiosyncratic
stress without central bank support. The internal target for the name-specific stress is a
90-day survival horizon.

Reporting. The liquidity position is reported daily to the Treasurer and weekly to ALCO.
The contingency funding plan is tested annually through a simulation exercise involving
Treasury, Finance and Risk.

Intraday liquidity. Intraday liquidity is monitored against the largest expected net
outflow in the Reserve Bank Information and Transfer System (RITS), with a buffer of 20%.
