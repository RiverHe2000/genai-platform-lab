# Operational Risk and Resilience (CPS 230)

Critical operations. The bank has identified the following critical operations: payments
processing, deposit-taking, lending origination and servicing, and customer enquiries
through the contact centre and digital channels.

Tolerance levels. The Board has approved the maximum tolerable period of disruption for
each critical operation: payments processing 4 hours, internet and mobile banking 8 hours,
and the contact centre 24 hours. Tolerance levels are reviewed annually.

Notification to APRA. A disruption to a critical operation that exceeds its tolerance
level, or is likely to, must be notified to APRA as soon as possible and no later than 72
hours after the bank becomes aware of it.

Material service providers. A register of material service providers is maintained and
reviewed annually. The bank must notify APRA within 20 business days of entering into or
materially changing an agreement with a material service provider.

Business continuity. Each critical operation has a business continuity plan that is tested
at least annually, including at least one scenario involving the failure of a material
service provider.

Risk assessment. Operational risk and control self-assessments are performed at least
annually for every business unit, and control effectiveness is tested by Operational Risk
on a rotating basis.
